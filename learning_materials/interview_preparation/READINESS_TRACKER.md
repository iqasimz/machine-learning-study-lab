## Minimum defensible skill claim

At least one independent implementation/change, a failure you can diagnose, a correct mechanism explanation and a saved result appropriate to the claim. Safety/label leakage failures block readiness even if other scores are high. No numerical total converts this tracker into a hiring probability.
